01 — about

I'm a MarTech Consultant based in Pune, working across Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Salesforce Data Cloud, and Pardot. Over five years I've moved from building journeys and emails to owning the full technical delivery platform setup, integration architecture, data modeling, and the campaigns that run on top of it.

I sit between business requirements and the platform itself: gathering what stakeholders actually need, then designing SFMC and Data Cloud solutions that hold up in production. That's meant data migrations, identity resolution, near real-time ingestion, and journeys triggered off live behavioral events not just batch sends.

I also mentor junior developers and write the documentation that keeps a MarTech implementation maintainable after I've handed it off. Deliverability, governance, and compliance are part of the job, not an afterthought.
